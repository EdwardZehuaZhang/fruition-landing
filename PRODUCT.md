# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Operations, RevOps, and IT leaders — plus founders of mid-size businesses — in Australia, the UK, the US, and APAC who are evaluating a consulting partner for monday.com, HubSpot, Atlassian, or AI implementation. They arrive from search (SEO/AEO is the primary acquisition channel), they are comparing several partners at once, and they are looking for two signals fast: *does this firm actually build things* and *what will it cost me*. The job to be done is de-risking a hire: they want evidence of competence, plain pricing, and a low-friction way to book a call.

## Product Purpose

fruitionservices.io is a ~100-page marketing and content site whose single conversion goal is a booked consultation (Calendly). It exists to convert search traffic into qualified calls by demonstrating engineering-grade competence in plain language. Success looks like: consistent design across every page, every page usable on a phone, and no page undermining the credibility the best pages establish.

## Brand Personality

**Precise, technical, approachable.** The voice of an engineer who explains clearly, not a marketer who hypes. We measure, we score, we ship — stated in plain human language. Fixed fees are printed on the page. Numbers and specifics beat adjectives. Confidence without stiffness: a real team you could have a call with tomorrow.

## Anti-references

- **The AI-hype aesthetic.** No dark-mode neon, no sparkle icons, no glassmorphism, no "magic" language. Fruition sells AI implementation as engineering, and the design must treat it that way.
- **The Wix-era legacy drift.** The site's own past is the anti-reference: mixed fonts per page, four competing button styles, hardcoded one-off layouts, desktop-only pages. Any new page that invents its own design system is a regression.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what we preach.** A workflow-systemization consultancy's website must itself look systemized. Cross-page consistency is not polish — it is the sales pitch.
2. **Evidence over adjectives.** Lead with stats, fixed prices, named regions, real client outcomes. The mono-labeled data moments (pricing tables, stat strips, log panels) are the brand's proof-of-work.
3. **One system, many pages.** Every page is composed from shared tokens and components. No page-local fonts, colors, or button variants — ever.
4. **Mobile is a first-class client.** Three breakpoints (mobile <768, tablet ≥768, desktop ≥1024); type steps down one size on mobile. A page that only works on desktop is not done.
5. **Content is sacred.** The redesign changes presentation only. Copy, structure, and Sanity content are SEO assets and are never removed or rewritten as part of visual work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast against its background (including text on the purple accent and on dark panels), large text ≥3:1. Touch targets ≥44px on mobile. Every animation carries a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative. Focus states visible on all interactive elements. Light theme only (dark mode is disabled site-wide by design).
