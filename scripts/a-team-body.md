### Angela

This lead intake agent will qualify leads as they come in. The trigger is adding a new Item on the monday Leads Board. Here's what Angela can do:

- Researches the company through web searches and the website.
- Scores the lead between 0 and 100 along with 5 weighted dimensions.
- Creates the full Lead Intelligence Document.
- Promotes ≥70 to CRM and flags lower scores with a reason.

### Ned

Angela will hand off to Ned, the CRM Engagement Agent. The trigger? New Item created with a 'New' status. The capabilities of this AI Agent include the following:

- Reads the Lead Intelligence Document end-to-end before drafting or writing.
- Checks Google Calendar for three available slots.
- Sends a personalised outreach email with a Calendly link.

### Maggie

Ned hands off stuff to Maggie. This AI Agent will propose a presentation brief. Its trigger is Status → Generate Proposal. Maggie can do the following:

- Reads Lead Intelligence Document from the CRM item.

- Writes Gamma-ready prompts to the CRM columns.

### Marge

Once handover is done, Marge comes in as a Proposal Builder Agent. It works on the trigger Status → Prepare Proposal Details. Marge has capabilities like the following:

- Reads the notes, CRM documents, and meeting transcripts.
- Writes a complete 7-section proposal to the CRM column.
- Sets the status to 'Generate Proposal' for Make.com.

### Bart

Marge hands off to Bart, the Project Kick-off Agent. Bart's trigger is CRM Status → Won. This monday AI Agent comes with functionalities such as the following:

- Reads the proposal: payment, timeline, scope, stakeholders, etc.

### LISA

Once handed to LISA, this AI Agent continues to monitor the loop. It's the Project Manager Agent with the trigger Weekdays 8 AM, Fridays 4 PM. LISA can do the following:

## The End-to-End Flow: The A Team's Pipeline Explained

According to [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/sales/topics/sales-pipeline), 75% of B2B buyers prefer sales rep-free experiences. Adding an AI Agent to the sales workflow layer can enhance pipelines.

Fruition's 6 Agent workflow on monday.com, The A Team, ensures an end-to-end flow through the full sales pipeline. Our AI Agents will handle everything from first contact to active delivery. Every stage will be automated and connected. Let's take a look at the flow:

1. **Capture**: A new lead arrives from any source, such as WorkForm, Apollo.io, or HubSpot.
2. **Qualify**: Angela will research and score between 0 and 100 in your Drive.
3. **Engage**: Ned will reach out to the lead and schedule calls using Fireflies.ai.
4. **Propose**: Proposal and deck will get drafted for you by Marge and Maggie using Claude and Gamma.
5. **Sign**: Documents will then be sent for review and signature from your sales team using DocuSign and tracked to close.
6. **Kick-off**: Bart will spin up the project the moment it's 'Won' using Calendar integrations.
7. **Deliver**: LISA will monitor the sales pipeline health and share reports with your team.

The Capture and Engage stages include external integrations. Similarly, the Propose stage is a Make.com Scenario. Sign will involve human touchpoints. And Qualify, Kick-off, and Deliver will be done by the 6 AI Agents.

## The monday.com Workspace Infrastructure

Four monday.com Boards will power the entire sales pipeline. The A Team can analyse data from the first lead right down to the live project portfolio.

### Leads Board: For Intake

WorkForm intake that's compatible with any lead source. Angela will qualify the leads before they reach your CRM. The key columns include lead score, Intel Doc, company website, industry, deal value, and qualification status.

### Deals, Agent Solution: The CRM Hub

This is your full CRM with 8 status stages. Every integration touchpoint lives here. Examples include Google Drive, DocuSign, Gamma, and Fireflies. Key columns include the status pipeline, Google folder, DocuSign and signed doc, envelope ID, transcriptions, Gamma prompt and link, and proposal details.

### Portfolio, Agent Solution: For Delivery

Bart will create a portfolio item for every 'Won' deal. It mirrors progress and the actual timeline from the linked project board. LISA will then use it for daily health monitoring.

### Knowledge Entries: Shared Reference

This will be your structured knowledge base. It's consumed by all agents — pricing tiers, policies, sales scripts, and the company profile. It gets segmented by audience: Internal, AI Agents, or both.
