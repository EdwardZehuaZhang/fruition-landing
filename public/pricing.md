## How pricing works

Three rules we never break.

- Fixed fee per phase. Every phase has a published price agreed before work starts. If we mis-scope it, that is our cost, not yours.
- Defined outcomes. Each phase ends with a named deliverable: a roadmap, a live system, a trained team. You always know what you are buying.
- Regional pricing. AUD for Australia, GBP for the UK, USD for the US: priced for each market, same deliverables everywhere.

## Pricing FAQs

### Does Fruition charge hourly rates?

No. Fruition works on fixed-fee phases with defined outcomes. Hourly billing rewards slowness; fixed fees reward outcomes. Managed service retainers are the only recurring pricing, and they carry defined SLAs.

### What if the project scope changes mid-phase?

Scope changes are re-quoted as a new fixed price before any additional work begins. Nothing is billed that was not agreed in writing.

### How much does consulting cost with Fruition?

Fruition consulting engagements start at AUD $15,000 for a structured initial phase, with fixed-fee pricing published per phase. Mid-scope projects are quoted after a scoping call. We serve Australia (AUD), the UK (GBP) and the US (USD) with local pricing in each region.

### Which regions does Fruition deliver consulting services in?

Fruition delivers from Sydney (headquarters, serving APAC including Singapore and India), London (UK and Europe), and New York (US and Canada). Engagements are delivered remotely as standard, with optional on-site workshops in each region.

### How long does a typical consulting engagement take?

Most consulting engagements run 4 to 12 weeks from kickoff to delivery depending on scope. Fruition works in fixed phases with defined outcomes, so you always know what is being delivered, by when, and at what cost.

### Why choose Fruition over a Big-4 consulting firm?

Fruition is practitioner-led: the person who scopes your engagement is the person who delivers it. We publish fixed fees, hold 500+ implementations of delivery history, and are certified partners across monday.com (Platinum), Atlassian, HubSpot, and the major AI platforms, so recommendations are cross-platform and honest.

### Can Fruition deliver this remotely?

Yes. All Fruition services are delivered remotely as standard across Australia, the UK and the US, with optional on-site workshop weeks in Sydney, London and New York.
